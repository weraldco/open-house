## HTML

## LINKS

**_absolute link_** is an outside source link, like google.com, meta.com

**_relative link_** is a link inside in you website/ relative to your site, for example the navbar, menu

**_blank_** open in new tab.

**_id="#top"_** go to div with an id of top
